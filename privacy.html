Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Aureila Talent ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website, why, and your rights. You can contact us at Aureilamanagement@outlook.com.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in the audit and application forms: a name or stage name, your email address, links to your profiles, your answers to our questions, and the boundaries you choose.
  • Anything you choose to tell us in free text boxes or by email.

We ask only for what we need. You never have to give us your legal name to enquire.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send your audit, and to reply to your application or enquiry.
  • To respect your boundaries if you become a client.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the people at Aureila Talent who need it to respond to you. Our forms are processed by Formspree, which receives your form submissions on our behalf. Booking links, if you use one, are provided by a scheduling service and are subject to its own policy.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

If you do not become a client, we keep your enquiry for up to 12 months in case you get back in touch, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
