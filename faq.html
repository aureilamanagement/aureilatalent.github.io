questions

Good questions, honest answers

Can I be anonymous?

Yes, absolutely! We work with all kinds of creators, and you never have to show your face or share your real name publicly.

I have not got an account yet. Can I still apply?

Yes, of course! We can help you with the setting up process.

What makes you different?

We are entirely female owned and run, and we build you a brand, not just send traffic to your page. We are big on boundaries and big on your comfort in the industry, and we protect you in every way we can.

Is my content and information kept private?

Yes. Only your dedicated manager has access, and everything is covered by our agreement.

What is a free account audit?

A free 15 minute review of your main traffic platform. We look at what is holding back your growth and give you something useful, whether or not you decide to work with us.

Do you help with leaked content?

Yes. We offer DMCA takedown services, which means we monitor for leaked content and file takedown requests on your behalf. We cannot promise every copy will be removed, but we act quickly and keep at it.

Do I have to show my face?

No. Many creators choose to stay fully anonymous, and we plan your content and brand around what you are comfortable with.

How much does it cost?

We talk through how we work and our pricing on a call, so you know exactly what to expect before you decide anything.

Do you work with male creators?

No. We work entirely with female creators. It is what by women, for women means to us.

Still unsure?

Book a free account audit, or email us. We are happy to answer anything.
