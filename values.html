our values

The stars we steer by

These are not marketing lines. They are the structure of how Aureila is run. Tap a star to read more.

  1. You always know what we are doing, why we are doing it, and how it is going. No surprises, and no vague updates.

  2. This industry can be hard on people. We choose to be kind, to creators and to each other, in everything we do.

  3. Your boundaries are agreed at the start and adhered to every time. They are not a negotiation and they are not a sales tactic.

  4. Plain language, honest answers and regular check ins. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

  5. Our vision is set clearly from the top and shapes who we work with, who we hire and how we train. Everyone follows the same standards.

  6. We speak about creators and about each other with respect. Creators are people, never products.

An entirely female owned and run agency, built so that creators feel safe, heard and in control.
